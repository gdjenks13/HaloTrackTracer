Help

Scale. Pick the Scale tool, click across one lane edge to edge, enter its real width (4 wu) and press Apply.

Tracing. Add a path, click where it starts, then click ahead of the end. Each section starts where the last one ended and keeps its direction. A straight runs as far as your click (the first one also sets the heading). A turn with Set angle uses the angle you type and your click sets its radius; with Free it bends to wherever you click.

Editing. Drag a section's end dot to resize it. Drag the diamond in the middle of a section to bend it: a turn's angle and radius follow the cursor, and a straight pulled off its line becomes a turn (drop it back on the line to straighten it). Everything after it follows.

Point mode. Pick Point on the board (E) and click anywhere: each click is the next point, in any direction. Drag a point and only the two lines touching it move. Drag a diamond and that line bends between its two points while everything else stays put. On a loop, click the start dot to close it. Untick 5° steps for any angle.

Turn presets. With Turn and Set angle, pick 30, 45, 60, 90, 135 or 180. Type a Radius and every turn uses it, so your click only picks left or right; leave it empty and your click sets the radius.

Free curves (Shift). Hold Shift while dragging and the section becomes a free Bezier curve. Shift+drag a diamond: the start and end stay put and only the line between them bends. Shift+drag an end dot: the start and the diamond stay put and the end moves. The rest of the track stays where it is, so a corner can form where the curve meets the next section; it is circled on the board, and Smooth in Active path removes it. Make arc turns a curve back into a turn.

Deleting. Right-click a dot or press Del to delete a section; later sections join the one before. Type exact values in Active path.

Width. Set one width for a whole path in Active path, or give an open path a start and end width. To widen and shrink along the way, use the Width tool and click the path where it should change: the track goes in a straight line from its width before to the width you set there, then on to the next.

Games. Pick Halo 4 (Forge Island pieces) or Halo: Reach (Forge World pieces) in Blocks. The prefab works in stock Halo Map Studio: prefab import the file, then prefab place it in a map variant for that game.

Square to Halo. With 5° steps on, 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° line up with HMS's axes as long as you place the prefab without yaw.

Keys. T trace · S straight · R turn · E point · G grade · W width · J jump · C scale · P pan · F fit · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc cancel.
